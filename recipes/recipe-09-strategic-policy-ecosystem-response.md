# Recipe 9 — Develop strategic, policy and ecosystem response

> **Decision question:** What should the NMCA do next?

## Goal

Translate analysis into a practical NMCA response. This recipe turns the outputs
of earlier recipes into strategic positioning, policy recommendations and
capability or ecosystem actions.

## When to use this recipe

- Once a role is chosen and gaps are understood.
- When advising government or executives on a coordinated response.
- When a technically mature NMCA seeks ecosystem leadership.

## Framework modules operationalised

- Strategic response
- Policy response
- Capability / ecosystem response

## Ingredients / required inputs

- Preferred role and business case summary
- Gap register and value pathway
- Institutional scan
- Leadership priorities and stakeholder engagement evidence

## Method / step-by-step process

1. Summarise the selected role, value pathway and viability decision.
2. Develop strategic options: lead, partner, advise, convene, support or defer.
3. Identify policy changes or standards commitments needed.
4. Define capability investments and ecosystem actions.
5. Create a phased roadmap with owners, milestones and evidence checkpoints.
6. Prepare communication outputs for executives, government partners or stakeholders.

## Templates / tools used

- Strategic and policy roadmap
- Role options matrix
- Business case evidence sheet

## Outputs / plating up

- Strategic response statement
- Policy response
- Capability and ecosystem roadmap
- Executive briefing
- Communication plan

The phased roadmap and its evidence checkpoints set up the monitoring in Recipe 10.

## Quality checks and evidence notes

- The roadmap has owners, milestones and evidence checkpoints.
- Policy and standards commitments are specific and actionable.
- Communication outputs are tailored to each audience.
