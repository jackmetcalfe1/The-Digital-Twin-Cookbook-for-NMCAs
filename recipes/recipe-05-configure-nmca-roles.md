# Recipe 5 — Configure possible NMCA roles

> **Decision question:** What role, or combination of roles, should we consider?

## Goal

Identify the role, or combination of roles, that the NMCA could legitimately and
feasibly play in the specific Digital Twin case. The recipe avoids assuming that
the NMCA must always lead.

## When to use this recipe

- After capability (Recipe 3) and institutional context (Recipe 4) are understood.
- When choosing how — not just whether — to engage.
- When a clear hand-off to partners may be more appropriate than leading.

## Framework modules operationalised

- Feasible role configuration

## Ingredients / required inputs

- Use-case brief
- Geospatial requirements profile
- NMCA capability baseline
- Institutional context scan
- Stakeholder needs and partner landscape

## Method / step-by-step process

1. List possible roles: foundational data provider, standards steward, quality
   assurer, technical advisor, platform/service provider, convenor, policy
   advisor and ecosystem steward.
2. Score each role against use-case requirements, capability fit, mandate fit,
   partner demand and risk.
3. Identify role combinations and hand-off points with other actors.
4. Eliminate roles that are outside mandate, disproportionate or unsupported by capability.
5. Select one preferred role and one fallback role.
6. Document why non-engagement or advisory-only engagement may be appropriate if evidence is weak.

## Templates / tools used

- Role options matrix
- Institutional context scan
- Use-case brief

## Outputs / plating up

- Role options matrix
- Preferred role
- Fallback role
- Role rationale
- Boundary conditions for engagement

Carry the preferred and fallback roles into value mapping and the business case.

## Quality checks and evidence notes

- Roles outside mandate or capability are explicitly eliminated, not left open.
- A genuine fallback role and a non-engagement option are both considered.
- Hand-off points to partners are named.
