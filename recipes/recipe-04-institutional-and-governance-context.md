# Recipe 4 — Analyse institutional and governance context

> **Decision question:** What mandate, governance and policy conditions enable or constrain action?

## Goal

Assess whether the wider authorising environment supports the NMCA role being
considered. This recipe recognises that technical capability is not sufficient
if mandate, policy, accountability or governance conditions are weak.

## When to use this recipe

- When responding to an incoming DT request from another public body.
- When advising government on national DT strategy.
- Whenever legitimacy, mandate or accountability is in question.

## Framework modules operationalised

- Institutional and governance context
- Legitimacy and accountability

## Ingredients / required inputs

- Legislation and organisational mandate
- National strategies and policy documents
- Governance structures and procurement rules
- Data-sharing agreements and licensing models
- Stakeholder map

## Method / step-by-step process

1. Map statutory responsibilities, mandate boundaries and strategic priorities.
2. Identify national Digital Twin, data, AI, infrastructure, climate or spatial data initiatives.
3. Map relevant public bodies, regulators, commercial actors and research partners.
4. Assess data-sharing, licensing, procurement and privacy constraints.
5. Identify who can authorise, fund or block NMCA action.
6. Record legitimacy and accountability risks.

## Templates / tools used

- Institutional context scan
- Stakeholder and beneficiary map

## Outputs / plating up

- Institutional context scan
- Authorising environment map
- Governance constraints and enablers
- Legitimacy / accountability risk register

Use the authorising environment map to constrain which roles are realistic in Recipe 5.

## Quality checks and evidence notes

- Mandate boundaries are cited to legislation or formal strategy, not assumed.
- Authorising actors (who can fund or block) are explicitly named.
- Accountability and legitimacy risks are logged, not glossed over.
