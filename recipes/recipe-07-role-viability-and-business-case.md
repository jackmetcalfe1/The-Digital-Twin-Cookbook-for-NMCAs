# Recipe 7 — Test role viability and business case

> **Decision question:** Is the preferred role feasible, fundable and proportionate?

## Goal

Test whether the preferred role is feasible, fundable and proportionate. This
recipe treats the business case as a public-sector decision argument, not only a
financial return calculation.

## When to use this recipe

- Before committing to a role or investment.
- When responding to an incoming request and needing a short business case.
- When checking whether a proposed project is really a DT (with Recipes 1 and 2).

## Framework modules operationalised

- Role viability and business case

## Ingredients / required inputs

- Preferred role and value pathway map
- Capability baseline and cost estimates
- Funding options and delivery partners
- Risk register and policy constraints

## Method / step-by-step process

1. Estimate implementation, maintenance and opportunity costs.
2. Identify funding route, delivery model and partnership model.
3. Assess benefits, risks and dependencies.
4. Compare preferred role with fallback role and non-engagement.
5. Assess procurement, licensing and sustainability implications.
6. Make a **go, adapt, pause or reject** recommendation.

## Templates / tools used

- Business case evidence sheet
- Role options matrix
- Risk, assumption and uncertainty register

## Outputs / plating up

- Business case summary
- Viability score
- Delivery model
- Funding and partnership assumptions
- Go / adapt / pause / reject decision

Present the business case summary and explicit recommendation to decision-makers.

## Quality checks and evidence notes

- Opportunity cost and the non-engagement option are assessed, not just the preferred role.
- Funding and partnership assumptions are stated and testable.
- The recommendation is one of go / adapt / pause / reject, with reasoning.
