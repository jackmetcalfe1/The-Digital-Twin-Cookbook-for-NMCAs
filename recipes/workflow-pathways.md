# Workflow pathways for different NMCA situations

The cookbook is navigable by **decision situation**, not only by recipe number.
Find the row that best matches your situation and follow the recommended recipe
sequence. The recipes are modular, so adapt the sequence to your evidence and
mandate.

| NMCA situation | Likely decision need | Recommended sequence | Expected final output |
|----------------|----------------------|----------------------|-----------------------|
| Exploring Digital Twins for the first time | Understand whether DTs are relevant and where to start | **1 → 2 → 3 → 10** | Use-case shortlist, geospatial complexity profile, readiness baseline and learning plan |
| Responding to an incoming DT request from another public body | Decide whether and how to engage quickly | **1 → 2 → 4 → 5 → 7** | Role recommendation and short business case: support, advise, partner, defer or reject |
| Selecting pilot DT use cases | Identify high-value, feasible pilots | **1 → 2 → 3 → 6 → 7** | Prioritised pilot list with value, feasibility and capability evidence |
| Building a case for investment in DT capability | Justify funding for new capability, data services or standards work | **2 → 3 → 6 → 7 → 8 → 9** | Investment case, capability gap register and phased roadmap |
| Advising government on national DT strategy | Clarify NMCA role in national policy, standards and governance | **4 → 5 → 6 → 8 → 9** | Policy advice, role configuration and ecosystem response roadmap |
| Small or resource-constrained NMCA | Identify realistic roles without overextending capacity | **1 → 2 → 3 → 5 → 8** | Selective engagement plan, partnership options and quick-win capability actions |
| Technically mature NMCA seeking ecosystem leadership | Move from data/service provision to stewardship or convening | **4 → 5 → 6 → 7 → 9 → 10** | Ecosystem stewardship case, policy commitments and monitoring plan |
| Commercially oriented or semi-commercial NMCA | Balance revenue opportunities with public value and mandate | **1 → 4 → 5 → 6 → 7 → 9** | Business / public value alignment statement and role viability decision |
| Unsure whether a proposed project is really a Digital Twin | Avoid relabelling conventional GIS or dashboard projects as DTs | **1 → 2 → 7** | DT necessity decision and minimum viable requirements statement |
| Post-pilot review | Decide whether to scale, adapt or stop | **6 → 7 → 8 → 10** | Outcome review, revised value pathway, updated gap register and scale/adapt/stop recommendation |

## How to read a pathway

- **Sequence is guidance, not a rule.** Skip a recipe if you already hold a
  validated output for it; revisit an earlier recipe if new evidence invalidates
  its assumptions.
- **Outputs chain together.** Each recipe produces an output used by the next, so
  keep the completed templates as your evidence trail.
- **Stop early when the decision is clear.** If Recipe 7 returns a confident
  "reject", you do not need to run the remaining recipes.
