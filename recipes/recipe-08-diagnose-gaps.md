# Recipe 8 — Diagnose capability, governance and policy gaps

> **Decision question:** What prevents the preferred role from being delivered well?

## Goal

Convert the evidence collected in earlier recipes into a prioritised set of
barriers that must be addressed before the NMCA can act effectively.

## When to use this recipe

- After a role and business case have been provisionally chosen.
- When building an investment case that must justify closing specific gaps.
- For small or resource-constrained NMCAs identifying quick wins.

## Framework modules operationalised

- Capability, governance and policy gaps

## Ingredients / required inputs

- Geospatial requirements profile
- Capability baseline
- Institutional context scan
- Role matrix and business case summary
- Risk register

## Method / step-by-step process

1. Compare geospatial requirements against NMCA capabilities.
2. Compare preferred role against mandate and authorising environment.
3. Identify technical, organisational, governance, policy and ecosystem gaps.
4. Classify gaps as quick wins, medium-term improvements or strategic investments.
5. Identify dependencies and owners.
6. Prioritise gaps using urgency, impact, feasibility and risk.

## Templates / tools used

- Gap register
- DT-Geospatial Complexity Index scoring sheet
- NMCA readiness and capability assessment

## Outputs / plating up

- Prioritised gap register
- Dependency map
- Mitigation actions
- Evidence needs
- First draft of capability and policy roadmap

The prioritised gap register feeds directly into the strategic response in Recipe 9.

## Quality checks and evidence notes

- Each gap traces back to evidence from an earlier recipe.
- Gaps are classified by horizon (quick win / medium / strategic) and have owners.
- Prioritisation uses explicit criteria (urgency, impact, feasibility, risk).

## Suggested next recipes

- **Recipe 10 — Review, iterate and monitor outcomes** (for post-pilot review)
